TrueOffer — Fake Offer Letter & Phishing Inspector
===

Description
---
TrueOffer is an AI-powered security scanner designed to inspect job offer letters, emails, and links to detect employment scams, recruitment fraud, and fake companies before job seekers lose money or sensitive data.

Problem It Solves
---
Job seekers frequently fall victim to recruitment scams involving fake job offers that demand upfront fees use artificial urgency, or mimic well-known company domains. TrueOffer identifies these deceptive social engineering tactics and unverified domains.

Key Features
---
-Text & URL Scanning: Analyzes pasted offer letter text, email messages, or suspicious job portal links.
-AI Fraud Detection: Uses Google Gemini AI and pattern matching to detect red flags like upfront fees, deposits, and pressure tactics.
-Domain Age Check: Checks domain registration age to catch newly created phishing and impersonation websites.
-Threat Score & Recommendations: Delivers an instant 0–100% risk score with highlighted evidence and safety advice.
